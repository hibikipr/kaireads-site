Kai Reads

Space Phonics & Reading Fun

Join Kai on a reading adventure across four planets. Sound out words, build spelling, catch sight words, and read stories — with every word spoken aloud.

📲 Coming soon to the App Store iPhone & iPad

Four planets. Four reading skills.

Each planet is a bite-sized game built around a skill early readers need.

🦖

Dino Planet

Sound it out — tap each letter to hear its sound, then BLAST the asteroid!

👻

Haunted Moon

Build words — drag letter tiles into order to spell the word.

🕸️

Web Station

Catch sight words — quick rounds of the words readers know by heart.

📖

Story Planet

Read a story — short space tales read aloud, line by line.

Stars, stickers & goals

⭐

Every win earns a star

Earn 5 stars in a game to unlock its collectible sticker in Kai's Sticker Book. Progress syncs to Game Center so it's never lost.

🎯

Parent-set daily & weekly goals

Set a star goal in Parent Settings and Kai cheers when it's hit — practice becomes a small, repeatable win instead of open-ended screen time.

🗣️

Natural speech, no internet needed

Every word is spoken aloud with on-device voices. Parents can tune speed and pitch, and use Apple's best downloaded voices.
