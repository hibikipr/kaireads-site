Stars, stickers & goals

⭐

Every win earns a star

Earn 5 stars in a game to unlock its collectible sticker in Kai's Sticker Book. Progress syncs to Game Center so it's never lost.

🎯

Parent-set daily & weekly goals

Set a star goal in Parent Settings and Kai cheers when it's hit — practice becomes a small, repeatable win instead of open-ended screen time.

🗣️

Natural speech, no internet needed

Every word is spoken aloud with on-device voices. Parents can tune speed and pitch, and use Apple's best downloaded voices.
